Relay: Smart Call Forwarding — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Relay: Smart Call Forwarding (“Relay”, “the app”) is an Android app made by Kozo Technology that forwards your calls to another number through your mobile carrier, manually or automatically, and can screen incoming calls. This policy explains what information the app and its advertising partner handle, and the choices you have.

Contact: kozo.technology@gmail.com

Information the app stores on your device

Relay forwards calls by sending your carrier the standard forwarding codes. It has no account and no server, and it does not send any of the following to us or anyone else. Everything stays on your phone:

  • your presets: names, destination numbers, and the icon, colour or contact photo you chose
  • your schedules, smart triggers (car Bluetooth, Wi‑Fi, calendar, battery, Do Not Disturb) and call-screening rules
  • the activity log of forwarding changes, with destination numbers shortened, kept for 90 days
  • for call screening: a one-way scrambled code of each caller's number (so a repeated call within 15 minutes can be recognised) and its last four digits, kept for 90 days
  • your settings, and when the optional 5-day unlock ends

Uninstalling Relay deletes all of it. Android's own backup may copy presets, schedules, the log and settings to your Google account, as it does for any app; you control that in Android's settings. Note that uninstalling does not cancel forwarding at your carrier: use “Cancel all forwarding” in the app first.

Calendar and contact information is read on your device only and is never transmitted. Forwarded calls are handled by your carrier; your plan's charges for forwarded calls may apply.

Advertising and your consent

This app shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, and an optional rewarded video that you choose to watch to unlock the smart features for 5 days. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and operating-system information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the app asks for your consent through Google's certified consent management platform. Personalised ads are shown only if you consent; otherwise you may see limited, non-personalised ads, or no ads.
  • United States: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Change your choice at any time: you can change or withdraw consent at any time in the app's Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device's Google settings.

Watching the rewarded ad is always your choice. Manual forwarding, timers, weekly schedules, presets, the widgets, the Quick Settings tile, the activity log and the status check are free and work without it.

Children

Relay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
